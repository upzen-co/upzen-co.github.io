Made for people
who track things.

Your steps live in one app, your spreadsheets in another, your bank balance in a third. Charts puts every number you care about on one screen.

Everything in one place

Apple Health metrics, CSV and TSV files, and live REST APIs — side by side on one dashboard. Overlay any two series and see how they move together.

Actually private

No servers, no accounts, no analytics — there isn't even a sign-up screen. Your data stays on your device, full stop. This page has no trackers either.

Feels native

Pure Swift and Swift Charts, built for iOS 26 and macOS 26. Interactive scrubbing, widgets, haptics, dark mode — it feels like Apple made it.
